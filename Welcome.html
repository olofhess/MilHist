About MilHist

MilHist is built as a structured historical database rather than a traditional website. Conflicts, events, people and places are connected in a relational model, allowing you to explore history as a network rather than a timeline.

The application supports advanced filtering and navigation, making it possible to move between events, participants and locations seamlessly. All data is based on open sources and has been curated and structured for clarity and consistency.

Artificial intelligence has been used as a development tool throughout the project — assisting with data structuring, validation and interface design — while all content is reviewed and controlled before publication.
